Tyre Retreading

Give Your Tyres
Another Life.

Tyre retreading replaces the worn tread of a reusable tyre casing with a new tread. It provides a practical way to extend tyre life, reduce waste and achieve greater value from every casing.

01

Inspection

Reusable casings are carefully inspected.

02

Buffing

Old worn tread is removed.

03

New Tread

High-quality tread materials are applied.

04

Curing

The new tread is bonded under controlled conditions.

05

Final Check

Each retread undergoes quality inspection.

Sustainability

More Mileage.
Less Waste.

Retreading supports a more sustainable approach to mobility by extending the useful life of tyre casings and reducing the demand for new tyre manufacturing.
